Profile

Internet Guild is a design and development practice, born to reduce. Websites, products and AI for founders who embrace simplicity. We make things simple. We keep only what matters.

Eleven years of practice. We design, build and launch.

Founded and led by Nico Teodoro. Born in Argentina. Raised on the internet. Grown in Azeroth.

Approach

Every detail is there for a reason. Color, type, space, and motion help people know where to go. They also give each product its own look. If something helps, we keep it. If it gets in the way, we take it out. The goal: easy to get, easy to use.

Design and code are one process. We work with you from the first idea to the day it goes live. We care about how it looks and how it works. People make the calls. AI helps us work faster.

Simple is not a style. It’s the standard.

Experience

Age 13
First jobs: websites for the game servers of local cybercafés. Mu Online, Lineage 2, Argentum Online. Design and code start here.
2015
Starts working on his own. First clients from other countries, through freelance websites. Design and code, all remote.
2020
During the pandemic, freelance work becomes a studio: Simplify Computer. Projects in fintech, crypto, health, investment, energy, and early AI.
2024
Simplify closes. Back to working alone. Fewer projects, more care.
2025
A trip to Japan. Kyoto, its culture, and the idea of doing more with less. Everything learned over the years comes together in one idea: simple is not a style. It’s the standard.
April 11, 2025
After a meeting in Kyoto, Internet Guild is born. Same craft, new name, one rule: keep only what matters.
Now
Building products for AI, fintech, crypto, health, and real estate companies, and for other agencies. Design and code, from first idea to launch.
