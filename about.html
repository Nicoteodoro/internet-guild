The Internet Guild is a Tier S design and code studio.
We make things simple.
We keep only what matters.

Every detail has a reason to be there.
Color, type, space, and motion help people find their way.
They also give each product its own style.
If something helps, we keep it. If it gets in the way, we remove it.
The goal: easy to understand, easy to use.

Design and code are one process.
We work with you from the first idea to the live product.
We care about how it looks and how it works.
People make the decisions. AI helps us work faster.

Why a guild?
In the old days, a guild was a group of people who shared the same work and the same high standards.
In online games, it’s the team you trust in the hardest fights.
We are both.

Founded and led by Nico Teodoro.
Born in Argentina.
Raised on the internet.
Grown in Azeroth.

Timeline

Age 13
First jobs: websites for the game servers of local cybercafés. Mu Online, Lineage 2, Argentum Online. Design and code start here.
2015
Starts working on his own. First clients from other countries, through freelance websites. Design and code, all remote.
2020
During the pandemic, freelance work becomes a studio: Simplify Computer. Projects in fintech, crypto, health, investment, energy, and early AI.
2024
Simplify closes. Back to working alone. Fewer projects, more care.
2025
A trip to Japan. Kyoto, its culture, and the idea of doing more with less. Everything learned over the years comes together in one idea: simple is not a style. It’s the standard.
April 11, 2025
After a meeting in Kyoto, The Internet Guild is born.
Now
Building products for AI, fintech, crypto, health, and real estate companies, and for other agencies.
