About

Internet Guild is a tier-s design practice, born to reduce.
Websites, products and AI for founders who embrace simplicity.
We make things simple. We keep only what matters.

Every detail is there for a reason. Color, type, space, and motion help people know where to go. They also give each product its own look. If something helps, we keep it. If it gets in the way, we take it out.

The goal: easy to get, easy to use.
Design and code are one process.

We work with you from the first idea to the day it goes live.
We care about how it looks and how it works.
People make the calls. AI helps us work faster.

Eleven years of practice. We design, build and launch.

Founded and led by Nico Teodoro.
Born in Argentina.
Raised on the internet.
Grown in Azeroth.

Timeline

Age 13 First jobs: websites for the game servers of local cybercafés. Mu Online, Lineage 2, Argentum Online. Kids paid to play, servers needed a face, and someone had to design and code it. That someone was him. Design and code start here, long before they had a name.

2015 Starts working on his own. No office, no team, no studio. Just a laptop and freelance platforms. The first clients come from other countries: briefs in English, payments in dollars, everything remote. Design and code, end to end. The craft hardens. The studio begins without a name.

2020 The pandemic hits. Freelance work turns into a studio: Simplify Computer. A team, more clients, more noise. Projects in fintech, crypto, health, investment, energy, and early AI. The work gets bigger. The calendar fills. Something about it starts to feel heavy.

2024 Simplify closes. Back to working alone. Fewer projects, more care. The noise drops. The work gets quieter, and better. The question is no longer how much to take on, but what is worth keeping.

2025 A trip to Japan. Kyoto, its culture, and the idea of doing more with less. Temples, empty rooms, things that last because nothing extra was added. Everything learned over the years comes together in one idea: simple is not a style. It’s the standard.

April 11, 2025 After a meeting in Kyoto, Internet Guild is born. Same craft, new name, one rule: keep only what matters.

Now Building products for AI, fintech, crypto, health, and real estate companies, and for other agencies. One person. Design and code. From first idea to launch. The same kid who built game-server sites for cybercafés, still making things for the internet, just cleaner.
