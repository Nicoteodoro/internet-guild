Internet Guild is a Tier-S design and code studio.
An essentialist practice, built to reduce.

We design, build, and launch websites, interfaces, and products for founders who choose simplicity.

Eleven years of practice. Take everything, or just what you need.

Founded and led by Nico Teodoro. Born in Argentina. Raised on the internet. Grown in Azeroth.

Timeline

Age 13
First websites in HTML and CSS for neighborhood cybercafés — the ones running their own servers for Mu Online, Lineage 2, and Argentum Online. Design and code start here.
2015
Goes independent. Begins working with companies abroad through freelance platforms. Design and engineering, shipped remotely.
2020
In the middle of the pandemic, freelance becomes a studio: Simplify Computer. Work spans fintech, crypto, web3, health, finance, venture capital, HR, energy, and early AI.
2024
Simplify closes. Practice returns to independent work — tighter, more deliberate.
2025
A trip to Japan. Kyoto, culture, and a philosophy of less. The page turns. Years of craft get poured into one idea: simplicity as a standard, not a style.
April 11, 2025
After a meeting in Kyoto, Internet Guild is founded.
2025 — 2026
Shipping products for AI, fintech, crypto, health, real estate, partner agencies, and companies that want less noise and more signal.
