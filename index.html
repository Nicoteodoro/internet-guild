About

Founded and led by Nico Teodoro.
Born in Argentina. Raised on the internet.
Grown in Azeroth.

He made his first websites as a kid, for the online games at his local cybercafés. He never stopped. Over the years he worked alone, built a studio, closed it and started again. Along the way he learned one simple thing: good work is not about adding more. It’s about knowing what to leave out.

Age 13. First websites, for the game servers of local cybercafés: Mu Online, Lineage 2, Argentum Online. Design and code start here.

2015. Goes solo. Just a laptop and clients from other countries, all remote.

2020. Freelance becomes a studio: Simplify Computer. A team, more clients, more noise.

2024. Simplify closes. Back to working alone. Fewer projects, more care.

2025. A trip to Japan. In Kyoto, one idea sticks: simple is not a style. It’s the standard.

April 11, 2025. After a meeting in Kyoto, the guild is born.

Now. Websites and products for AI, fintech, crypto, health and real estate companies, and for other agencies. The same kid from the cybercafés, still making things for the internet. Just cleaner.
